## Services

**You receive:** a fully linked 3-statement model (your actuals + 5-year forecast) with an assumptions page you control, DCF valuation with a WACC build, trading comparables, a WACC × growth sensitivity grid, Bear/Base/Bull scenario toggle, dashboard with charts, a checks panel that must read PASS, plus a 2-page PDF summary and a 5-slide financial deck extract.
**Turnaround:** 4–7 days · **From:** $295 · **See it:** [Showcase 01 — CloudLedger SaaS model](showcase-01-saas-model/)

Available as a one-off build or a monthly retainer.
**Turnaround:** 5–7 days for the build, then monthly · **From:** $495 build / $500 per month refresh

### 3. Financial Model Audit, Fix & Rescue
**The problem:** An investor, lender or buyer is about to diligence your model — and you don't fully trust it. Hidden hardcodes, broken links and a balance sheet that quietly doesn't balance are the norm in inherited models, and almost nobody sells a standalone fix.
**You receive:** a findings report (every error located by sheet and cell, rated Critical/Major/Minor, with its financial impact quantified), the corrected workbook, and a checks dashboard that stays green. Fixed fee, agreed before work starts.
**Turnaround:** 48–72 hours · **From:** $175 · **See it:** [Showcase 02 — model audit](showcase-02-model-audit/)

### 4. Options & Trading Analytics — Code & Research
**The problem:** Your TradingView strategy shows 000 P&L and no trades execute; or you have rules in your head and no honest way to know how they behaved historically, what they cost in slippage, or what your maximum loss on a position actually is.
**You receive:** your rules, coded — Pine Script strategies/indicators or Python backtests and options payoff/Greeks tools — with commented source code that you own, realistic transaction costs and slippage in every backtest, walk-forward splits, and a tear-sheet report (equity curve, drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino, parameter sensitivity).
**Turnaround:** 1–3 days (Pine) · 3–7 days (Python) · **From:** $150
*This is a software and research service. I code and test your rules; I do not provide trading signals, investment advice, or any expectation of profit. Past performance does not guarantee future results.*

### 5. Equity Research Pack + AI-Research QA
**The problem:** You need buy-side-quality company analysis — or your AI drafting tools produce fluent research with invented numbers, broken causal chains and valuations that don't foot to any model.
**You receive:** an initiation-style report built on public data (business, industry, financial history, forecast, DCF + comps with sensitivity, catalysts, risks, full sourcing), the linked company model behind every number in the report, and — for research teams — annotated QA passes on AI-generated drafts that flag factual, causal and valuation errors.

*Reports are general-information work products on public data, not personalised recommendations.*
